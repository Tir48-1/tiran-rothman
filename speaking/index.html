Behavior, Money & Decision Making

Keynotes, executive talks and academic lectures that connect behavioral economics with practical financial decisions.

By Dr. Tiran Rothman · Updated October 2026
DIRECT ANSWER

Dr. Tiran Rothman speaks on behavioral economics, investor psychology, financial freedom, valuation and decision making. The talks combine academic research with practical examples from markets, investing and corporate finance, and can be adapted for executives, investors, universities and general audiences.

Behavioral Economics in Everyday Life

Why do intelligent people make choices they later regret? This talk uses framing, social influence, reference points, overconfidence and attention to show how behavioral economics appears in ordinary decisions — from pricing and negotiation to consumption and career choices.

Financial Freedom in an Irrational World

Financial freedom is not only an investment problem. It is a problem of time, consumption, identity and behavior. The lecture develops a practical framework for thinking about how money can buy optionality and time, why lifestyle inflation is powerful, and how decision systems can matter more than isolated financial tips.

The Psychology of Investing

Markets create an unusually demanding environment for decision making: outcomes are noisy, feedback is delayed and social information is everywhere. This talk explores herd behavior, fear of missing out, loss sensitivity, overconfidence and narrative thinking — and then translates those ideas into portfolio and decision rules.

Valuation Under Uncertainty

For founder, investor and executive audiences, this session explains why startup valuation is not simply a multiple applied to revenue. It connects operating assumptions, scenarios, market evidence, financing terms and dilution, and shows how to distinguish a valuation model from a fundraising narrative.

Formats

Sessions can be delivered as keynotes, executive workshops, university lectures, panels or tailored company sessions. Content can be adapted for finance professionals or for audiences without a technical background. Speaking is available in English and Hebrew.
