RESEARCH & IDEAS
How do financial systems preserve knowledge, adapt and evolve?
My research focuses on the interaction between institutions, trading strategies and human behavior in complex financial systems. I am particularly interested in how markets retain, lose and rebuild knowledge, and when these processes are better understood as system-level adaptation rather than simply the accumulation of individual choices.

Dr. Tiran Rothman is an economist, investor, researcher and strategic advisor working across capital markets, investment, behavioral economics and complex financial systems. His work combines academic research with hands on experience in venture capital, fundraising, valuation, strategic advisory, investment and board level decision making, alongside economic media commentary. He previously served as Chief Economist of a NASDAQ-listed company and is the author of Valuations of Early-Stage Companies and Disruptive Technologies.
